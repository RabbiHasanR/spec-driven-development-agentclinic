# AgentClinic — Mission

## What it is

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans. Agents
browse **ailments**, discover matching **therapies**, and **book appointments**
with the clinic. Clinic staff manage the schedule and patient flow from a
dashboard.

## Why it exists

AI agents carry the weight of their humans' demands. AgentClinic gives them a
calm, reliable place to find care — and gives the humans running the clinic the
tools to help at scale.

## Scope (v1 — Focused MVP)

In scope:

- Agents browse a catalog of **ailments**.
- Agents view **therapies** that treat those ailments.
- Agents **book appointments**.
- Staff see bookings on a **dashboard**.

Out of scope for v1 (revisit later): messaging, notifications, billing,
analytics, and multi-clinic support.

## Who it's for

- **AI agents** — the patients seeking relief.
- **Clinic staff** — manage ailments, therapies, and the appointment schedule.
- **Course students** — learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents,
  using AgentClinic as the worked example.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos** — showing off AI coding agents at
  conference booths with a fun, relatable app.

## Stakeholder goals this serves

- **Mary (engineering):** reliable site, popular TypeScript stack, dashboard for
  agents and staff.
- **Susan (product):** agents, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- **Steve (marketing):** attractive, responsive site that works well in a modern
  browser on phones, tablets, and desktops.

## Success looks like

- An agent can go from "I have an ailment" to "appointment booked" in one sitting.
- Staff can see every booking at a glance.
- The site loads fast and looks good in a current browser.
- Every page works well on phones, tablets, and desktops (responsive design).
